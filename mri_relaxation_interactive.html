Signal ∝ (1 − exp(−TR/T1)) × exp(−TE/T2), assuming a 90° excitation, full spin-echo refocusing, equal proton density, and idealized tissue constants. Illustrative T1 values: white matter 350 ms, gray matter 700 ms, selected to demonstrate maximal longitudinal recovery separation at approximately 485 ms. These are not representative in-vivo 3T tissue values. This is a teaching model, not a simulation of MPRAGE or gradient-echo BOLD.

Important: T1 recovery curves show longitudinal magnetization, which is not directly measured by the receive coil. The subsequent RF excitation converts available longitudinal magnetization into transverse magnetization. T2 curves show the decay envelope of transverse magnetization, related to the measured spin-echo amplitude. Both TR and TE are defined for both T1- and T2-weighted acquisitions.
